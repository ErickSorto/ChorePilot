Share the chores, remember less, and make room for a calmer home. ChorePilot gives families, couples, and roommates one place to plan housework and see what’s done.

A shared plan for real life
• Organize chores by room, add instructions, and assign who does what.
• Schedule one-time or recurring tasks and rotate responsibilities.
• Get reminders for upcoming and overdue chores, with notification controls.

A little help getting started
Take a room photo to get AI chore suggestions. Review and edit the ideas before adding them to your plan. You choose what makes sense for your home.

Meet your chore buddy
Pick a squirrel, penguin, or cat. Completed chores help your virtual pet earn XP and level up—a little encouragement for everyday effort.

See the work behind a tidy home
Request completion photos when a chore needs a check. Household admins can review the result, approve it, or ask for another photo. Follow completed, planned, and overdue tasks in household reports.

From unloading the dishwasher to the weekly bathroom clean, give every chore a place in the plan.
