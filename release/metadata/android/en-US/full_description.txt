Housework is easier when everyone knows what needs doing. ChorePilot is a chore planner and household task tracker for families, couples, and roommates. Keep a shared plan, build a cleaning routine, and see what gets done.

Find chores with a photo
Not sure where to start? Use a room photo to get AI chore suggestions. Review and edit the ideas before adding them to your household plan. You decide what makes sense for your home.

Share chores, room by room
Create a shared chore list for the kitchen, bathroom, living room, and more. Add instructions and assign tasks so everyone knows what to do and who is doing it.

Make a routine that fits
Set up one-time tasks or recurring chores. Choose repeat schedules and rotate assignments among household members to share the work over time.

Meet your chore buddy
Choose a squirrel, penguin, or cat as your virtual pet. Completed chores help your companion earn XP and level up, adding a little encouragement to everyday routines.

Keep track with photo proof
For chores that need a check, request a completion photo. Household admins can review submissions and approve the work or ask for another photo.

Remember less
Get reminders for chores that are coming up or overdue, plus updates on assignments and photo reviews. Choose which notification categories you want to receive.

See your effort add up
Check household reports for completed, planned, and overdue tasks. Follow progress over time and make the work behind a tidy home easier to see.

From unloading the dishwasher to the weekly bathroom clean, give everyday housework a place in the plan.
